## Idea
The idea is based on an article that I red a couples weeks ago , that talked about the fact that there is a prox time in
which a player usually gain a specific rank , the article was talking about valorant , so the idea is to create
something similar but for every skill , you wanna master so that you can build the habits of raking up your skill.

## Business Logic 
The app will present multiple rank level with the following subdivision
- Iron -> is the base level
- Bronze -> is hitten at the reach of the 50h on that skill.
- Silver -> is hitten at the reach of the 120h on that skill.
- Gold -> is hitten at the reach of the 300h on that skill.
- Platinum -> is hitten at the reach of the 600h on that skill.
- Diamon -> is hitten at the reach of the 1200h on that skill.
- Master -> is hitten at the reach of the 2400h on that skill.
- GrandMaster -> is hitten at the reach of the 4800h on that skill.
- Titan -> is hitten 10000h on that skill.

The there will be also achievements for things like streak and certian numbers of hours on a specific day.
